Clinical and academic focus

Precision care for rare, overlapping neurologic phenotypes.

Pediatric Neuroimmunology

Clinical interest in autoimmune encephalitis, MOG antibody-associated disease, pediatric multiple sclerosis, vasculitis, neurosarcoidosis-related inflammation, and complex inflammatory presentations.

Neurogenomics

Focus on monogenic mimics of neuroinflammatory disorders, white matter disease, whole-exome and whole-genome sequencing, RNA sequencing, and diagnostic frameworks for genetically mediated neurologic disease.

Care Transitions

Developing lifespan approaches for rare neuroimmune and leukodystrophy care, with an emphasis on continuity from pediatric diagnosis into adult neurologic follow-up.

Education Innovation

Building immersive and practical educational tools, including virtual-reality neuroanatomy curricula designed to reduce neurophobia among learners.

Neuroimmunology Neurogenomics Alliance

NINGA connects clinicians and researchers around difficult cases.

NINGA is a developing collaborative forum for rare disorders at the intersection of immune dysfunction, genetics, and white matter disease. The goal is to move from variant discovery toward mechanism, shared learning, and multicenter research readiness.
