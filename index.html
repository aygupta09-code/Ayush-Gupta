Clinical and academic focus

Clinical areas.

Pediatric Neuroimmunology

Care and consultation for children, adolescents, and young adults with inflammatory disorders of the brain, spinal cord, optic nerves, and peripheral nervous system, including relapsing demyelinating disease and autoimmune encephalitis.

  • Pediatric and adolescent multiple sclerosis
  • MOG antibody-associated disease, optic neuritis, and relapsing MOGAD
  • Neuromyelitis optica spectrum disorder
  • NMDA receptor encephalitis
  • Seronegative autoimmune encephalitis
  • Other pediatric autoimmune encephalitis syndromes
  • Post-infectious neuroinflammation
  • Transverse myelitis, ADEM, and relapsing demyelinating syndromes
  • CNS vasculitis, neurosarcoidosis, and complex inflammatory mimics

When appropriate and available, families may be connected with official clinical research opportunities through Arkansas Children's Research Institute .

White Matter Genetic Disorders and Rare Disease

Evaluation of pediatric and adult patients with genetic, metabolic, and immune-mediated rare diseases affecting white matter, motor pathways, cognition, and neurologic development.

  • Leukodystrophies and genetic leukoencephalopathies
  • Undiagnosed white matter disorders
  • Inborn errors of immunity with neurologic involvement
  • Monogenic mimics of neuroinflammatory disease
  • Rare neurogenetic and neuroimmune overlap syndromes
  • Adult-presenting or lifespan white matter disorders
  • Whole-exome, whole-genome, and RNA-sequencing interpretation in clinical context

General Child Neurology

General pediatric neurology evaluation and longitudinal management for children with motor disability, tone disorders, headache syndromes, and hereditary neurologic conditions.

  • Cerebral palsy
  • Hereditary spastic paraparesis
  • Spasticity management
  • Baclofen pump care and coordination
  • Pediatric migraine and headache disorders
  • Complex pediatric neurologic presentations requiring diagnostic synthesis

Neuroimmunology Neurogenomics Alliance

NINGA connects clinicians and researchers around difficult cases.

NINGA is a developing professional forum for rare disorders at the intersection of immune dysfunction, genetics, and white matter disease. The goal is to move from variant discovery toward mechanism, shared learning, and multicenter research readiness.
