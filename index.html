Clinical areas

Precision care for rare, overlapping neurologic phenotypes.

Pediatric Neuroimmunology

Evaluation and care for pediatric and adolescent multiple sclerosis, MOG antibody associated disease, optic neuritis, NMOSD, NMDA receptor encephalitis, seronegative autoimmune encephalitis, other pediatric autoimmune encephalitis syndromes, post-infectious neuroinflammation, transverse myelitis, ADEM, relapsing demyelinating disorders, and complex inflammatory mimics.

When available and clinically appropriate, patients may be considered for clinical trials through Arkansas Children's Research Institute.

White Matter Genetic Disorders and Rare Disease

Clinical interest in leukodystrophies, genetic leukoencephalopathies, undiagnosed white matter disorders, inborn errors of immunity with neurologic involvement, monogenic mimics of neuroinflammatory disease, and rare neurogenetic-neuroimmune overlap disorders in pediatric and adult patients.

My academic work emphasizes careful phenotype review, sequencing interpretation, and moving from genetic variants toward mechanism and care planning.

General Child Neurology

Care for cerebral palsy, hereditary spastic paraplegia, spasticity management, baclofen pump coordination, pediatric migraine and headache disorders, and complex pediatric neurologic diagnostic questions.

I value clear communication with referring clinicians and families while protecting formal clinical pathways for patient care.

Neuroimmunology Neurogenomics Alliance

NINGA brings rare neuroimmune and neurogenomic cases into sharper focus.

NINGA is a developing professional forum for rare disorders at the intersection of immune dysfunction, genetics, and white matter disease. The goal is to move from variant discovery toward mechanism, shared learning, and research readiness.
